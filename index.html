THE ARTIST

I'm a makeup artist with over five years of experience and a genuine passion for creating looks that make people feel confident and beautiful. Although I enjoy all aspects of makeup, I'm especially drawn to editorial, creative, and full glam makeup, where I can express my creativity and attention to detail.

I love the creative freedom that makeup offers—whether it's enhancing someone's natural features or completely transforming them through bold, artistic concepts. Every client and every project is unique, and I enjoy bringing each vision to life.

I'm constantly learning, exploring new techniques, and looking for opportunities to work on creative, editorial, and fashion projects while continuing to grow as an artist.
